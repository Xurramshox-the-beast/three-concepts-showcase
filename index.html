New Tashkent

A dense, vertical, green city concept

Back to overview ↑

Concept

A concept for Tashkent as a very dense, very tall, very green city of 38 million people, built inside the city’s real outline on the same 435 km². Instead of spreading outward, it grows upward and has everything in every district. About 60 metro lines run on five levels (deep, underground, ground, elevated and a monorail loop), with buses, commuter rings and suburban lines reaching out to the farmland. Sky bridges link the towers, the roofs are green, and vending machines stand on the street corners. The government buildings are spread across the districts on top of skyscrapers, and the parking is built into the lower floors of towers. Buildings shrink gradually toward the outskirts until only farmland remains. Underneath is a hollow hydraulic level of springs and water reservoirs. There are no ports or heavy water industry, so the space is saved. The main advantage is that people can reach nearly everything close to home or by public transport, so there are fewer traffic jams.

MAIN ADVANTAGE

People can reach nearly everything close to home or by public transport, reducing the need for car trips and helping limit traffic jams.

Urban edgeTowers gradually become shorter and give way to farmland
Underground conceptA hollow hydraulic level with springs and water reservoirs

How to explore: Use the layer buttons to explore the city systems. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and use the view controls to change the presentation.

Bucky

A universal home helper, guard and firefighter

Back to overview ↑

Concept

Bucky is a universal home robot: a helper, a guard and a firefighter in one. He is about 1.3 m tall, rides on wheels, and has two jointed arms. In chores mode he carries a tray, serves and wipes surfaces. In guard mode he scans the house with his searchlight and siren. In fire mode he turns toward the fire, extends a nozzle from the 20 L tank on his back, and puts it out. His main advantage is that one machine covers the three jobs a household needs most, comfort, security and safety, so you don’t need separate devices for each.

MAIN ADVANTAGE

One machine combines everyday assistance, home security and fire response, instead of requiring separate devices for each job.

At a glance

Main modesChores, guard and fire

How to explore: Select Chores, Guard or Fire in the model panel to see Bucky change mode. Drag to rotate and scroll or pinch to zoom.

INTERACTIVE 3D MODEL

iHome

A compact one-room home in a box

Back to overview ↑

Concept

iHome is a compact one-room home in a box, about 5.2 × 3.3 × 2.7 m outside, with roughly 14 m² of living space. It has a kitchenette, a double bed, a sofa and TV, a desk, a wardrobe, and everyday household items. It is not tiny like a capsule and not oversized like a flat. The main advantage is that everything a person needs fits in a small, efficient footprint, which makes it cheap to build, easy to place, and a good fit for a dense city like New Tashkent.

MAIN ADVANTAGE

A complete everyday living setup fits into a small footprint, making the unit straightforward to build and place in a dense city.
